Option A with chips · the rail for information, chips for actions

What the lead knows and its ideas behind the rail; "Ask the lead to" as chips at the message box

The rail keeps two icons: what the lead knows, and ideas (with their number); a click slides that card in over the edge of the chat, another click, Escape or a click elsewhere closes it. "Ask the lead to" leaves the rail: its three suggestions are chips just above the message box, one click puts the text in the box. Later panels (costs, the project's agents, approved commands) can join the rail without touching the chat. Here the ideas are open.
